# B1.1 — Audit the regular Galaxy S10 (beyond1lte) hardware

Status: **doing — public source audit started; no regular S10 available for live validation**

## Goal

Establish a source-backed hardware baseline for the Exynos Galaxy S10
(`beyond1lte`, SM-G973F family) before changing the existing S10e-oriented
`Beyond0LtePkg`. Keep the S10e measurements and assumptions visibly separate.
Do not promote a value to VERIFIED for the regular S10 without a source that
actually identifies `beyond1lte`, or a live read from that model.

## Confirmed from model-specific sources

- LineageOS device metadata identifies `beyond1lte` as Galaxy S10, models
  SM-G973F, SM-G973F/DS and SM-G973N, with Exynos 9820, 8 GB RAM, 128/256 GB
  storage and 3040x1440 display:
  https://github.com/LineageOS/lineage_wiki/blob/main/_data/devices/beyond1lte.yml
- Samsung vendor board DTS:
  `arch/arm64/boot/dts/samsung/exynos9820-beyond1lte_common.dtsi`
  includes `exynos9820-beyond2lte_mst.dtsi` and the S10-specific input
  include. It explicitly overrides UFS CAL board type to 16 (UNIV).
- S10 input DTS
  `exynos9820-beyond1lte_input_common.dtsi` identifies the touch project as
  BEYOND1/G973, firmware `tsp_sec/y771_beyond1_phole.bin`, TCLM level 2,
  AFE base 0x0206, and FOD support.
- Shared button DTS
  `exynos9820-beyondlte_input_common.dtsi` lists KEY_WINK on gpa0-6,
  VOLUME DOWN on gpa0-4, VOLUME UP on gpa0-3 and POWER on gpa2-4. Each
  button has a 10 ms debounce. The GPIO flag cell is copied as source data;
  its polarity/electrical meaning must be decoded from the binding and pinctrl
  before firmware uses it.
- The S10 board DTS binds DSIM to `s6e3ha9_beyond1`, uses gpa3-4 for the
  panel reset/power GPIO, and its panel lookup lists 1440x3040 L-cut/U-type
  variants plus a 1440x2960 temporary panel. The exact panel revision must
  not be guessed from the model name alone.
- The common Exynos 9820 DTS describes UFS at HCI 0x13D60000, vendor HCI
  registers at 0x13D61100, UniPro at 0x13D50000, UFS protector at
  0x13D70000, PHY/PMA at 0x13D64000, I/O coherency at 0x13C10700 and CPORT
  at 0x13D68000. It declares HCI hw-rev 6, two active lanes and Gear 3.
  These are SoC DT values, not yet live measurements on a regular S10.
- The same SoC DTS describes DWC3 at 0x10C00000 with IRQ SPI 0xF1,
  USB DRD PHY at 0x10B00000 and auxiliary PHY windows 0x10AE0000 and
  0x10AF0000. The S10 board override selects OTG and SuperSpeed Plus and
  supplies Samsung DWC3 quirks and PHY tuning references. Host-mode electrical
  safety, role-switch policy, VBUS and charging behaviour remain separate
  work; do not infer them from `dr_mode = "otg"`.

## Explicitly not established

- No regular S10 is connected or available for live validation.
- The S10e live RAM bank extents, framebuffer base, bootloader handoff state,
  panel timing/variant, partition geometry and UFS DMA aperture are not
  transferable assumptions.
- The S10e-specific `EXYNOS_FB_BASE`, 1080x2280 geometry, touch tuning and
  memory reservations must not be carried into a regular-S10 target.
- The S10 panel DTS offers multiple 1440-wide variants; framebuffer address,
  stride, pixel format and actual selected panel revision remain unverified.
- Button GPIO values above are source-derived only. GPIO controller base,
  pinmux, polarity, wake behaviour and interrupt routing still need a full
  source trace before any UEFI input implementation.
- USB MMIO/PHY addresses are source-derived only. Clock/reset sequencing,
  PMIC/VBUS, Type-C orientation/mux, role-switch and charging interactions
  need separate evidence.
- UFS addresses are not sufficient to initialise the controller. CAL board
  type, PHY sequencing, regulator/reset/clock setup, FMP/SMU/IOMMU, DMA limits,
  LUN enumeration and device geometry must be traced independently.

## Next actions

1. Complete a line-referenced audit of the S10 board DTS, shared SoC DTS,
   input/pinctrl files and the exact UFS/USB/display driver initialisation
   sequences.
2. Compare every existing `Beyond0LtePkg` and `Exynos9820.h` assumption
   against those S10-specific sources; classify each as shared SoC fact,
   S10e-only fact, S10-specific fact or UNVERIFIED.
3. Design the smallest package/configuration change that targets `beyond1lte`
   without modifying the Exynos 9810 packages or disguising S10e constants.
4. Build what can be built in this environment and record the required Windows
   toolchain steps. Do not claim hardware validation.
5. Keep all flash/install actions out of scope until a regular S10 is available
   and a recovery route is established.
